Let God Be True — Whose Word Makes You a Child?

You become the child of the voice you keep receiving. That is why Jesus could say, 'You are of your father the devil.'

Watch this moment

What he said

Romans 3:4, All men are liars. Let God be true. So even if your thoughts make sense and they're from a man or a woman, isn't it a lie? So if you trust the lies of the enemy, you become the enemy. If I trust the word of God, aren't I a child of God? Yes. But if I trust the word of Satan, don't I become the child of Satan? And that's why he says in John 8:44, You are of your father, the devil. What? I thought God created everybody. Yeah, but when you accept the word of somebody, you become that. So if you keep saying I do to I'm a sinner, I do to I'm dirty, I do to I can't be loved, I do to I have to perform, I have to work out my salvation — then the inheritance gets functionally taken away in your daily walk.

He sets a hierarchy of truth: God is true; every human is a liar. Therefore even plausible, gender-neutral, internally consistent thoughts lose if they contradict God. Trusting the enemy's word aligns you with the enemy; trusting God's word is how you live as a child of God. He reads John 8:44 as more than a shock insult: receiving a father's word is how you take a father's nature. God created all; spiritual parentage follows the accepted word. Then he lists vows that functionally move Numbers 36's inheritance in the daily walk: I'm a sinner, I'm dirty, I can't be loved, I must perform, I must work out my salvation as earning.

Every loaded word

Not every “the” and “and” — the claims, pictures, and slang that carry the sermon.

“All men are liars. Let God be true.”

Romans 3:4, echoing Psalm 51:4 / 116:11. When God and man conflict, God wins. 'Men' here is humankind — he adds 'man or a woman cuz they're one.'

Why it matters. Your most intelligent inner lawyer is still a human. Insight is not inerrant. Scripture is the higher oath.

“even if your thoughts make sense”

Coherence is not the test. Eden's lie made sense: fruit, wisdom, open eyes.

Why it matters. The most dangerous proposals are the ones that fit the evidence of your week.

“If I trust the word of God, aren't I a child of God?”

He ties sonship to received word — very Johannine (John 1:12; 8:31–47) and Petrine (1 Peter 1:23, born by the word).

Why it matters. This is not 'think positive, become God's kid.' It is: the new birth comes by the gospel word, and the family resemblance continues by staying in that word.

“I thought God created everybody”

He answers a real objection. Creation and fatherhood are not identical in John 8. Creator of all; father of those who abide in His word — or, devastatingly, of those who abide in the devil's.

Why it matters. This keeps the sermon from universalism and from racism. The split is not ethnicity (Jesus is talking to fellow Jews). The split is whose word you do.

“I do to I'm dirty… I can't be loved… I have to perform… work out my salvation”

A catalog of false vows. The last one alludes to Philippians 2:12, which he treats as a performance reading — 'I must earn/keep this by grind.'

Why it matters. Philippians 2:12–13 is in the Bible. The fight is over meaning: work out what God works in, versus work for a place at the table. The speaker is attacking the second.

Verses on the table

Romans 3:4 KJV

God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

Word by word

  • let God be trueAn imperative of allegiance. When accounts collide, God's account is the real world.
  • every man a liarNot 'people sometimes fib.' In this clash, humanity's self-defense against God is false.
  • justified in thy sayingsGod's words are the ones that will be shown right. Your job is to agree in advance.
How the speaker uses this verse

The kill-switch for 'but my thoughts are reasonable.' Reasonable still bows.

Wider context — keep the verse honest

Paul is defending God's faithfulness even if some Jews were unfaithful (Romans 3:1–3). The speaker universalizes the principle to inner thoughts. That is a valid pastoral use of a verse about God's truthfulness winning any dispute.

John 8:31–32, 44 KJV

If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free. … Ye are of your father the devil… When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

Word by word

  • continue in my wordAbide. Stay. Discipleship is remaining in a speech, not visiting it.
  • the truth shall make you freeFreedom is the fruit of a Father's word, not of self-loathing.
  • of your father the devilSpoken to people who claimed Abraham and even God as father (8:39–41) while seeking to kill Jesus. Family is proved by likeness and by which word you run with.
  • father of itLies have a genealogy. When you receive a lie, you are not being 'authentic.' You are being parented.
How the speaker uses this verse

His shock line: accepting a word makes you that one's child. He applies a verse aimed at murderously unbelieving leaders to the Christian's inner I dos. The mechanism (word → parentage) is Johannine; the original audience was not a struggling saint in small group. Use the mechanism, do not casually call a weak believer a child of the devil.

Wider context — keep the verse honest

This is one of the sharpest texts in the Bible. The speaker is riffing on a true principle (you resemble the word you receive) in a context that was about rejecting Jesus Himself. Be careful not to become a witch-hunter toward people who still feel dirty. The pastoral aim is: stop receiving the accuser as father. The original aim was: you reject me because you share the devil's desires.

Philippians 2:12–13 KJV

Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.

Word by word

  • Work outKatergazesthe: bring to the surface, carry to completion. Work out, not work for. Like working ore out of a mine you already own.
  • fear and tremblingSeriousness, not terror that you are unloved. The next verse is the reason you can even work.
  • God which worketh in youThe energy is His. Your 'I do' is cooperation with an inside God, not a ladder to an outside one.
How the speaker uses this verse

He lists 'I have to work out my salvation' among the false I dos when it means performance for belonging. He is right about that misuse.

Wider context — keep the verse honest

Do not throw the verse away. A bride works out the marriage in daily faithfulness because she is married, not so that the groom might finally like her. That actually fits the whole sermon.

Sit with this

Try the question before you open the answer. The answer is a companion, not a score key.

Question 1 · click to revealIf a stranger listened only to the father-voice you obey when you fail, would they guess the Father of Jesus — or a prosecutor who never rests?

Why this question hits

Children learn their name from the parent who talks most after a mess.

A way to answer

The Father of Jesus runs toward prodigals (Luke 15), convicts to restore, and calls the washed 'clean' (John 13:10). The prosecutor says dirty, unlovable, earn it. John 8 says the devil fathers lies. You may be a true child of God who still keeps the prosecutor on speed dial. That is not your nature in Christ. It is a received word you can evict.

Question 2 · click to revealWhich of the speaker's false vows is your native language: sinner, dirty, unlovable, performer, or salvation-earner — and who first taught you that dialect?

Why this question hits

These vows are usually inherited before they are theological. A home, a pulpit, a partner, a culture.

A way to answer

Naming the teacher of the dialect breaks some of its spell. If a parent only praised performance, 'I do to I have to perform' feels like love. If a church only preached Romans 3:23, 'I'm a sinner' feels like the whole Bible. Bring that origin to God. Let God be true and that first teacher a liar where they contradicted Him — even if they meant well, even if they were you.

Question 3 · click to revealGod created everyone. Why is that not the same thing as 'everyone is currently living as a child of God'?

Why this question hits

The speaker's 'what?' is our question. Creation-love can be used to dodge Jesus' hard line in John 8.

A way to answer

Creation means we all bear His image and owe Him our lives. Child, in John, means sharing the Father's nature, word, and work. You can be created by God and still refuse His Son, which is why Jesus can say both 'God so loved the world' and 'you are of your father the devil' without contradicting Himself. For the believer, the warning is smaller and closer: do not live like the old father still has custody of your inner speech.

Live it before the next session

  1. Memorize Romans 3:4 as a reflex for spirals: 'Let God be true.'
  2. Read John 8:31–47 and list every claim about fathers, words, and desires.
  3. Rewrite Philippians 2:12–13 in one sentence that cannot be used as a whip: e.g. 'I am bringing to the surface what God is already energizing in me.'
