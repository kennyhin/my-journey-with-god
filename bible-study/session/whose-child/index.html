Let God Be True — Whose Word Makes You a Child?

You become the child of the voice you keep receiving. That is why Jesus could say, 'You are of your father the devil.'

Watch this moment

What he said

Romans 3:4, All men are liars. Let God be true. So even if your thoughts make sense and they're from a man or a woman, isn't it a lie? So if you trust the lies of the enemy, you become the enemy. If I trust the word of God, aren't I a child of God? Yes. But if I trust the word of Satan, don't I become the child of Satan? And that's why he says in John 8:44, You are of your father, the devil. What? I thought God created everybody. Yeah, but when you accept the word of somebody, you become that. So if you keep saying I do to I'm a sinner, I do to I'm dirty, I do to I can't be loved, I do to I have to perform, I have to work out my salvation — then the inheritance gets functionally taken away in your daily walk.

He sets a hierarchy of truth: God is true; every human is a liar. Therefore even plausible, gender-neutral, internally consistent thoughts lose if they contradict God. Trusting the enemy's word aligns you with the enemy; trusting God's word is how you live as a child of God. He reads John 8:44 as more than a shock insult: receiving a father's word is how you take a father's nature. God created all; spiritual parentage follows the accepted word. Then he lists vows that functionally move Numbers 36's inheritance in the daily walk: I'm a sinner, I'm dirty, I can't be loved, I must perform, I must work out my salvation as earning.

Every loaded word

Not every “the” and “and” — the claims, pictures, and slang that carry the sermon.

“All men are liars. Let God be true.”

Romans 3:4, echoing Psalm 51:4 / 116:11. When God and man conflict, God wins. 'Men' here is humankind — he adds 'man or a woman cuz they're one.'

Why it matters. Your most intelligent inner lawyer is still a human. Insight is not inerrant. Scripture is the higher oath.

“even if your thoughts make sense”

Coherence is not the test. Eden's lie made sense: fruit, wisdom, open eyes.

Why it matters. The most dangerous proposals are the ones that fit the evidence of your week.

“If I trust the word of God, aren't I a child of God?”

He ties sonship to received word — very Johannine (John 1:12; 8:31–47) and Petrine (1 Peter 1:23, born by the word).

Why it matters. This is not 'think positive, become God's kid.' It is: the new birth comes by the gospel word, and the family resemblance continues by staying in that word.

“I thought God created everybody”

He answers a real objection. Creation and fatherhood are not identical in John 8. Creator of all; father of those who abide in His word — or, devastatingly, of those who abide in the devil's.

Why it matters. This keeps the sermon from universalism and from racism. The split is not ethnicity (Jesus is talking to fellow Jews). The split is whose word you do.

“I do to I'm dirty… I can't be loved… I have to perform… work out my salvation”

A catalog of false vows. The last one alludes to Philippians 2:12, which he treats as a performance reading — 'I must earn/keep this by grind.'

Why it matters. Philippians 2:12–13 is in the Bible. The fight is over meaning: work out what God works in, versus work for a place at the table. The speaker is attacking the second.

Verses on the table

Open each card for a word-level read, then how the speaker uses it, then the wider context so the verse is not kidnapped.

Romans 3:4

KJV
God forbid: yea, let God be true, but every man a liar; as it is written, That thou mightest be justified in thy sayings, and mightest overcome when thou art judged.

  • let God be true

    An imperative of allegiance. When accounts collide, God's account is the real world.

  • every man a liar

    Not 'people sometimes fib.' In this clash, humanity's self-defense against God is false.

  • justified in thy sayings

    God's words are the ones that will be shown right. Your job is to agree in advance.

John 8:31–32, 44

KJV
If ye continue in my word, then are ye my disciples indeed; And ye shall know the truth, and the truth shall make you free. … Ye are of your father the devil… When he speaketh a lie, he speaketh of his own: for he is a liar, and the father of it.

  • continue in my word

    Abide. Stay. Discipleship is remaining in a speech, not visiting it.

  • the truth shall make you free

    Freedom is the fruit of a Father's word, not of self-loathing.

  • of your father the devil

    Spoken to people who claimed Abraham and even God as father (8:39–41) while seeking to kill Jesus. Family is proved by likeness and by which word you run with.

  • father of it

    Lies have a genealogy. When you receive a lie, you are not being 'authentic.' You are being parented.

Philippians 2:12–13

KJV
Work out your own salvation with fear and trembling. For it is God which worketh in you both to will and to do of his good pleasure.

  • Work out

    Katergazesthe: bring to the surface, carry to completion. Work out, not work for. Like working ore out of a mine you already own.

  • fear and trembling

    Seriousness, not terror that you are unloved. The next verse is the reason you can even work.

  • God which worketh in you

    The energy is His. Your 'I do' is cooperation with an inside God, not a ladder to an outside one.

Live it before the next session

  1. Memorize Romans 3:4 as a reflex for spirals: 'Let God be true.'
  2. Read John 8:31–47 and list every claim about fathers, words, and desires.
  3. Rewrite Philippians 2:12–13 in one sentence that cannot be used as a whip: e.g. 'I am bringing to the surface what God is already energizing in me.'
