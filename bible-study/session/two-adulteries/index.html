Two Adulteries — and the One God Is Jealous For

Physical sin is real. The teacher says the covenant God is hunting is the spiritual one.

Watch this moment

What he said

Did you guys know there's two types of adultery? Spiritual adultery and also physical adultery. The one that God cares about is the spiritual one. Adultery is not idolatry. It's adultery. Meaning like sleeping around, changing, becoming one with somebody. I'll give you scripture. So true adultery isn't just sleeping with someone other than your husband. True adultery is cheating on the husband with the enemy. Christ died. He made us righteous. And then he grafted us together. We became one. As 2 Corinthians 11:2 tells us, espoused to be one husband, presented as a chaste virgin. So he is the bridegroom. We are the bride. We're one wife of God, basically. And then we turn around and we trust the thoughts and the words of the enemy. We trust the way of the world. We agree with the world and what it says about us and our identity, which is what Satan, the god of this world is. Little G. Not big G. So we agree with what Satan keeps whispering about our salvation specifically and our identity specifically.

He draws a hard line: there is body-adultery and spirit-adultery. He even refuses to let you rename it 'just idolatry,' because he wants the shock of the marriage bed — becoming one with somebody. Then he tells the gospel in four moves: Christ died, He made us righteous, He grafted us in, we became one. 2 Corinthians 11:2 is the wedding certificate. The betrayal is that the bride then believes the world's report about who she is, especially about salvation and identity. That report, he says, is the whisper of the 'god of this world' — Satan, little g.

Every loaded word

Not every “the” and “and” — the claims, pictures, and slang that carry the sermon.

“two types of adultery”

Physical: sexual breaking of a human marriage. Spiritual: giving a rival the union that belongs to God/Christ. Scripture uses both (Exodus 20:14; James 4:4; Jeremiah 3).

Why it matters. If you only preach one, you will either become a purity cop who misses the heart, or a mystic who shrugs at bodies. He is focusing the lens, not erasing the body.

“The one that God cares about is the spiritual one”

Overstatement for emphasis. God cares about both (1 Cor 6:18–20). His point: God is more jealous for exclusive worship and trust than our culture is.

Why it matters. Take the heat, not the exaggeration as a license. Spiritual betrayal is not 'the only sin God notices.' It is the one this sermon is here to name.

“Adultery is not idolatry. It's adultery.”

He refuses a softening word. Idolatry can sound like a museum sin. Adultery sounds like a spouse in the next room.

Why it matters. The prophets already mix the two (Jeremiah 3:6–9). He wants feeling, not a lexicon fight. Becoming one with a rival is the shared core.

“sleeping around, changing, becoming one”

His working definition: union that relocates you. Not only a moment of desire. A change of belonging.

Why it matters. This is why agreeing with a thought can count. Agreement is how you 'sleep around' with a word.

“Christ died. He made us righteous. He grafted us. We became one.”

Four-beat gospel: substitution, justification, inclusion (Romans 11 olive tree), union.

Why it matters. You cannot understand the affair if you skip the wedding. He is not shaming singles of Jesus. He is reminding the married bride.

“trust the way of the world… what it says about us and our identity”

The world is not only 'out there' culture. It is a voice about you: who you are, what you are worth, how you get clean.

Why it matters. This aims the rest of the sermon at identity, not at concert tickets and movies — though those can carry the same voice.

“god of this world… Little G. Not big G.”

2 Corinthians 4:4. Satan has borrowed jurisdiction over blinded minds, not equality with Yahweh.

Why it matters. He is protecting worship. You can take the world's verdict seriously as a weapon without baptizing Satan as a second God.

“whispering about our salvation specifically and our identity specifically”

The targeted lies are not only 'eat more cake.' They are 'you're not really His' and 'you are what you did.'

Why it matters. If the enemy can move those two pieces, he does not need to destroy the rest of your theology. You will live as if the cross bounced.

Verses on the table

Open each card for a word-level read, then how the speaker uses it, then the wider context so the verse is not kidnapped.

2 Corinthians 11:2

KJV
For I am jealous over you with godly jealousy: for I have espoused you to one husband, that I may present you as a chaste virgin to Christ.

  • godly jealousy

    The emotion of a faithful husband, not a controlling boyfriend. God wants you, not a piece of you.

  • espoused you to one husband

    Already betrothed. The church is not shopping. She is spoken for.

  • present you as a chaste virgin

    Paul's goal is a whole people, undivided. Chastity here is loyalty of mind and worship.

Romans 11:17–18

KJV
And if some of the branches be broken off, and thou, being a wild olive tree, wert graffed in among them, and with them partakest of the root and fatness of the olive tree; Boast not against the branches.

  • graffed in

    Inserted into a living tree you did not grow. Union with Israel's root, Christ, the covenant fatness.

  • partakest of the root

    You share sap. Righteousness is not a sticker on a dead branch. It is life from the root.

  • Boast not

    Grafting is mercy. The speaker's 'He grafted us together' must stay humble or it becomes spiritual racism or pride.

2 Corinthians 4:4

KJV
In whom the god of this world hath blinded the minds of them which believe not, lest the light of the glorious gospel of Christ, who is the image of God, should shine unto them.

  • god of this world

    theos of this aion. A title of function: he runs the present evil age's blinding system. Not a second Yahweh.

  • blinded the minds

    The war is illumination vs. darkness in the mind — same theater as this sermon.

  • them which believe not

    In this verse the blinded are unbelievers. The speaker extends the whisper-campaign to believers' identity. Other texts support that extension (2 Cor 11:3; Eph 6:11), but we should not rewrite 4:4's audience.
